Orbital — Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version: Orbital collects nothing. Orbital does not hold the Android internet permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no servers. Everything below happens entirely on your device.

What Orbital uses, and why

Your installed apps. Orbital is a home-screen launcher. It reads the list of launchable apps on your device (through Android's standard launcher queries) so it can display them. This list never leaves your device.

App launch counts. To size and order its bubbles, Orbital keeps a simple count of how often you open each app, stored only in Orbital's private storage on your device. It is never transmitted or shared, and it is deleted when you uninstall Orbital or clear its data.

Notifications. If you grant notification access, Orbital reads the app, title, and text of your current notifications so it can show notification badges and a brief preview that grows out of the app's bubble. Notification content is held in memory only while the notification exists, is never recorded or stored, and never leaves your device. You can turn notification access off at any time in Android Settings, and Orbital works without it (badges and previews simply stop).

Step count. If you grant the physical activity permission, Orbital reads your device's step counter to display it in a widget. Step data is displayed only, is not stored by Orbital beyond the current reading, and never leaves your device.

Data sharing

Orbital shares no data with anyone, because it collects no data and has no way to send any.

Children

Orbital is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children.
